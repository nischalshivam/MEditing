# Scene Brain Regression Constitution

1. Never treat guessed episodes or model timestamps as authority.
2. Never borrow across episodes to satisfy an exact event.
3. Use the same exact scene at most twice in a final project.
4. Never chop one long scene into consecutive short clips merely to hit pacing targets.
5. No video clip exceeds 10 seconds.
6. Relevance outranks cut frequency.
7. Weak evidence becomes `MANUAL_REQUIRED` or an explicit gap.
8. Verify source and transcript identity before use.
9. Every solved production bug receives a regression test.
10. Any failed mandatory assertion makes the global release gate fail.
11. Frozen projects and historical receipts remain immutable.
12. Do not build unrelated whole-series Rich indexes.
13. Do not repeat AI analysis for unchanged source/prompt/model evidence.
14. Human review is an acceptable production state.
15. False automatic confidence is never acceptable.
